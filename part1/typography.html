Why Machine Learning Matters

Machine learning allows computers to find patterns in data and improve their performance over time, without being explicitly programmed for every possible situation.

From recommendation systems to medical diagnosis, ML models are trained on large datasets so they can generalize to new, unseen examples. The quality of the data often matters as much as the algorithm itself.

Tip: Supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning are the three main paradigms every ML practitioner should understand first.
